Privacy & cookies.

Effective November 21, 2026. This notice applies to the JayNova Brands website and its free downloads. JayNova is an independent fictional creative project maintained through nadidoug/jaydabrands.

You can download the guide without an account, email address, payment, or optional analytics. We do not have an email signup form or send marketing email from this site.

What happens when you visit

The website serves its own page files, fonts from your device, and downloadable assets. We do not load advertising pixels, social media embeds, external web fonts, or third-party analytics scripts.

Our hosting provider may process ordinary request information, including IP addresses, for delivery, security, and operation. The public website is hosted through GitHub Pages. The hosting provider's own notice also applies to its processing.

Cookies and local storage

Our page does not set tracking cookies. It uses one local-storage item, jaynova_privacy_choice, to remember your optional analytics choice, the notice version, and when you chose it. This preference lasts up to 180 days unless you clear browser storage or change it.

Local storage is a browser technology similar to cookies. You can choose Essential only or open Cookie preferences at any time. Closing a preference window does not consent to analytics. Global Privacy Control and a Do Not Track signal keep our optional counting off.

Optional download counting

Only after you allow optional analytics, downloading the starter guide sends its product identifier, a consent flag, and the notice version to our Supabase backend. We do not send a name, email address, account ID, user agent, or page-referrer value in that event.

The database keeps a daily total of accepted download events. It does not maintain an individual visitor profile. Totals are approximate event counts, not unique people, customers, or sales.

For basic abuse prevention, the backend briefly processes the request's IP address in memory and stores a daily-changing keyed hash with an hourly counter. The raw address is not stored in our application tables. The hash is pseudonymous technical information and is not described as fully anonymous. Supabase may also process ordinary service logs under its own policies.

Who processes the information

The site operator maintains the project. GitHub Pages hosts the public website, and Supabase processes the optional counting requests and stores the application's counters. Providers may process data in the United States and other locations under their own terms. See GitHub's privacy statement and Supabase's privacy policy.

We do not sell personal information or use these counters for targeted advertising.

Your choices and questions

To stop future optional counting, choose Essential only in Cookie preferences. Clear browser storage to remove your saved preference. Previously aggregated totals cannot be linked back to an individual visitor. We cannot retrieve a person's activity from those totals.

For a general privacy question, contact the maintainer through a GitHub issue. GitHub issues are public: do not post IP addresses, email addresses, credentials, or other private information. The site does not collect sensitive information or intentionally target children.

Updates

If we add a signup form, payment processing, new tracking, or a different use of data, we will update this notice and the relevant choices before enabling it. This notice describes the current technical setup; it is not a certification of compliance with every jurisdiction's laws.
